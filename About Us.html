About Pretoria Home Electronics

Pretoria Home Electronics (Pty) Ltd is a local electronics retail organisation serving customers in Pretoria and surrounding communities.

Our purpose is to make reliable electronic products more accessible to customers by providing quality products, competitive prices and helpful customer service.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide customers with quality, affordable and reliable electronic products while delivering friendly and professional customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted electronics retailer in Pretoria, recognised for affordability, reliability, convenience and excellent customer service.

Our Values

  • Quality: We aim to provide reliable products.
  • Customer Service: We put customers first.
  • Affordability: We aim to provide competitive prices.
  • Integrity: We believe in honest communication.
  • Reliability: We aim to provide dependable products and service.

Who We Serve

Our target customers include families, students, working professionals, households, small businesses and anyone looking for electronic products for their home or workspace.
