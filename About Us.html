About Us

Get to know more about Pretoria Home Electronics and what we do.

Who We Are

Pretoria Home Electronics (Pty) Ltd is a small retail business based in Pretoria, South Africa. We specialise in supplying household electronics and accessories to customers looking for quality products at affordable prices.

Our product range includes televisions, sound systems, kitchen appliances, phone chargers, cables, remotes and other everyday electronic products.

We aim to become a trusted local electronics retailer by understanding our customers' needs and providing friendly, reliable service.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality and affordable electronic products while delivering excellent customer service. We want to make everyday technology accessible and convenient for households, students, working adults and families.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a recognised and trusted electronics retailer in Pretoria and surrounding communities through quality products, fair prices and long-lasting customer relationships.

Our Values

Quality

We aim to provide reliable products that meet our customers' everyday needs.

Customer Service

We believe in treating customers with respect and providing helpful assistance.

Affordability

We aim to offer products at fair and reasonable prices.

Integrity

We value honesty, transparency and responsible business practices.

Reliability

We strive to build customer trust through dependable service and product information.

Who We Serve

Our target customers include:

  • Households looking for home electronics.
  • Students who need affordable electronic accessories.
  • Working adults looking for convenient household appliances.
  • Families who want reliable home entertainment products.
  • Customers in Pretoria and surrounding suburbs.
